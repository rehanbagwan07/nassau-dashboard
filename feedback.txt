Hi, I'm Rehan Bagwan. This is my Factory Reallocation and Shipping 
Optimization project for Nassau Candy Distributor.

Nassau Candy currently assigns products to factories using fixed, old 
rules. My project looks at their real shipping data and recommends 
better factory assignments to save on shipping time and cost.

I built a dashboard where you can pick a product and see how it would 
perform if shipped from each of the 5 factories - lead time, distance, 
and profit margin. There's also a page comparing the current factory 
vs my recommended one, a full list of 50 recommendations ranked by 
impact, and a risk page that flags any recommendation that could hurt 
profit margins.

One thing I found while working on this - the Ship Date column in the 
data didn't match up properly with Order Date, sometimes off by years. 
Instead of ignoring it, I pointed this out in my research paper and 
explained how it affects the results, and suggested the data should be 
checked at the source.

That's my project - a working dashboard plus a research paper and 
executive summary explaining the analysis. Thanks for watching.
